# App Store “Western Europe only” × Mr. Blue Sky — study deck

Indie-dev meme: a screen recording of **App Store Connect → Pricing and Availability → Specific Countries or Regions** (same regional groupings as Apple’s media-availability UI: “Europe, Russia, and Central Asia”, etc.). Checkboxes flip on **one per beat** while **Mr. Blue Sky** plays. The joke is launching “globally” but only ticking wealthy Western European storefronts.

## Song

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Title** | **Mr. Blue Sky** |

| **Album** | *Out of the Blue* (1977) |
| **Sync cue** | After the long intro, the bouncy **staccato “ba-da” vocal section** (~1:02+) — that’s what edits are cut to |

## Suggested slide order

1. **What is this?** — App Store territory picker UI (screenshot).
2. **Background** — `01-background-unchecked.png` (the “canvas”).
3. **Audio** — name the track; play ~10s of the staccato section.
4. **Mechanic** — one checkbox per beat; show `02-meme-recreation-western-europe-checks.gif`.
5. **Punchline** — `frame-checked-western-europe.png` (Austria, Denmark, Finland, France, Germany, Ireland, Italy, Netherlands, Norway, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland).
6. **Why it lands** — “world launch” that skips US, UK, Asia, etc.; relatable to anyone who’s shipped an iOS app.
7. **Meta** — same song powers Guardians-style montages, Gru’s plan sync edits, etc. (Mr. Blue Sky is the shared **edit rhythm**, not this UI specifically).

## How the original is made

1. Screen-record App Store Connect availability (or reuse a static UI + click sounds).
2. Mark beats in Premiere/DaVinci/CapCut (`M` on each hit).
3. Trim each “check” to one beat (often ~8–12 frames at 30fps depending on edit).
