# Remedy — Exercise Shot List

**Total:** 30 exercises across 3 equipment tiers (catalog v3, migration 031)  
**Ideal:** 1 subject, neutral background (white/light gray/cream), minimal props  
**Format:** MP4, 1080p minimum, 30fps, horizontal (16:9) preferred  
**Duration per clip:** 30–90 sec (enough for 2–3 full reps)  
**PT spec (from PRD):** Clean form demo, 1–2 verbal cues, no talking head intro/outro

Cut in catalog v3 (do NOT film): Pelvic Tilt, Knee-to-Chest, Supine Trunk Rotation, Standing Back Extension, Prone Hip Extension.

---

## Camera Setup

| Shot Type | When to use |
|---|---|
| **Side profile** | Primary angle for most exercises (shows depth/range of motion) |
| **45° front-side** | Secondary for asymmetric exercises (bird dog, open-book) |
| **Overhead** | Optional for floor exercises (cat-cow, dead bug) |

Film each exercise at **2 angles minimum** (side + front-side). Edit to best angle per exercise.

---

## Tier 1 — Open Space (no equipment needed)

Film all mat/floor exercises together (exercises 1–19) in one session. Order them by body position to minimise getting up and down.

### Floor exercises — prone (face down)

### 1. McKenzie Press-Up ✅ (filmed)
- **DB ID:** `cccc0001-0000-0000-0000-000000000022`
- **Angles:** Side profile (essential — shows lumbar extension arc)
- **Duration:** 45–60 sec (8–10 reps, slow)
- **Cues:** Hips stay on floor the entire time. Press up on arms fully, then lower under control.
- **Props:** Mat

### Floor exercises — supine (face up)

### 2. Dead Bug
- **DB ID:** `cccc0001-0000-0000-0000-000000000007`
- **Angles:** Side profile (essential — shows arm/leg lowering), overhead optional
- **Duration:** 60 sec (4–5 slow reps each side, 3-sec lowers)
- **Cues:** Lower back stays completely flat — if it lifts, the range is too large. Lower the opposite arm and leg over a slow 3 count.
- **Props:** Mat

### 3. Glute Bridge
- **DB ID:** `cccc0001-0000-0000-0000-000000000008`
- **Angles:** Side profile (essential — shows hip height)
- **Duration:** 45–60 sec (10–12 reps)
- **Cues:** Drive through heels, squeeze glutes at top, ribs stay down (don't hyperextend the low back).
- **Props:** Mat

### 4. Single-Leg Glute Bridge
- **DB ID:** `cccc0001-0000-0000-0000-000000000026`
- **Angles:** Side profile (essential)
- **Duration:** 45–60 sec (8–10 reps each leg)
- **Cues:** Non-working leg stays extended at 45°. Same cues as bilateral — hips level at the top.
- **Props:** Mat

### 5. Supine Figure-4 Stretch (Piriformis)
- **DB ID:** `cccc0001-0000-0000-0000-000000000019`
- **Angles:** 45° from feet-side (shows crossed leg + pull)
- **Duration:** 45 sec (hold 20–25 sec each side)
- **Cues:** Active foot (flex the ankle), gently draw the free leg toward chest.
- **Props:** Mat

### 6. Supine Hamstring Stretch
- **DB ID:** `cccc0001-0000-0000-0000-000000000030`
- **Angles:** 45° from feet-side (shows leg extension)
- **Duration:** 40–50 sec (hold 25–30 sec each leg)
- **Cues:** Other leg stays bent with foot on floor. Straighten the knee until a pull is felt, not a burn.
- **Props:** Mat

### Floor exercises — side-lying

### 7. Clamshell
- **DB ID:** `cccc0001-0000-0000-0000-000000000027`
- **Angles:** Side profile facing camera (shows top knee opening)
- **Duration:** 40–50 sec (12–15 reps each side)
- **Cues:** Pelvis doesn't roll back. Top hip stays directly over bottom hip. Small range is correct.
- **Props:** Mat

### 8. Open-Book Rotation
- **DB ID:** `cccc0001-0000-0000-0000-000000000003`
- **Angles:** Overhead or 45° from head (shows rotation arc)
- **Duration:** 45–60 sec (6–8 reps each side, slow)
- **Cues:** Knees stay stacked together, eyes follow the top hand, breathe into the rotation.
- **Props:** Mat

### 9. Side Plank (Knees)
- **DB ID:** `cccc0001-0000-0000-0000-000000000009`
- **Angles:** 45° front-side (shows full body line)
- **Duration:** 45–60 sec (3–4 holds of ~10 sec each side — McGill-style dosing)
- **Cues:** Hips don't sag. Ear, shoulder, hip, and knee in one line. Hold ~10 sec per rep, lower, repeat.
- **Props:** Mat

### 10. Side Plank (Full)
- **DB ID:** `cccc0001-0000-0000-0000-000000000031`
- **Angles:** 45° front-side (shows full body line, feet stacked)
- **Duration:** 45–60 sec (3–4 holds of ~10 sec each side)
- **Cues:** Legs straight, feet stacked. Straight line from ankles to shoulders — hips lifted, no sag.
- **Props:** Mat

### Floor exercises — all fours

### 11. Cat-Cow Stretch
- **DB ID:** `cccc0001-0000-0000-0000-000000000001`
- **Angles:** Side profile (essential), overhead optional
- **Duration:** 45–60 sec (8–10 full cycles, tied to breath)
- **Cues:** Full arch (cow) on inhale, full round (cat) on exhale. Head follows the spine naturally.
- **Props:** Mat

### 12. Bird Dog
- **DB ID:** `cccc0001-0000-0000-0000-000000000006`
- **Angles:** Side profile (essential — shows full extension), 45° front-side
- **Duration:** 60 sec (3–4 reps each side, **10-sec hold at top** — McGill-style dosing)
- **Cues:** Trunk stays completely still. Don't hike the hip on the extended leg side. Hold the top position ~10 sec.
- **Props:** Mat

### 13. Child's Pose Hold
- **DB ID:** `cccc0001-0000-0000-0000-000000000018`
- **Angles:** Side profile
- **Duration:** 30–40 sec (slow deep breathing, sink deeper with each exhale)
- **Cues:** Arms reaching long, sit bones toward heels, forehead rests on floor.
- **Props:** Mat

### Standing exercises

### 14. Standing Hip Flexor Stretch (Half-Kneeling)
- **DB ID:** `cccc0001-0000-0000-0000-000000000004`
- **Angles:** Side profile (essential — shows pelvic tuck + forward lean), 45° front-side
- **Duration:** 45–60 sec (hold 25–30 sec each side)
- **Cues:** Tall spine, tuck the pelvis (posterior tilt) before leaning forward into the stretch.
- **Props:** Mat (for the knee)

### 15. Bodyweight Hip Hinge
- **DB ID:** `cccc0001-0000-0000-0000-000000000011`
- **Angles:** Side profile (essential — shows neutral spine + hip push-back)
- **Duration:** 45 sec (10–12 reps)
- **Cues:** Push hips back (not knees forward), long neutral spine throughout, soft knee bend.
- **Props:** None — optional: dowel rod or broomstick on the spine as a teaching cue

### 16. Bodyweight Squat
- **DB ID:** `cccc0001-0000-0000-0000-000000000028`
- **Angles:** Side profile, 45° front
- **Duration:** 45–60 sec (10–12 reps)
- **Cues:** Chest tall, weight in mid-foot, knees tracking over toes, controlled descent.
- **Props:** None

### 17. Single-Leg Romanian Deadlift (Bodyweight)
- **DB ID:** `cccc0001-0000-0000-0000-000000000029`
- **Angles:** Side profile (essential — shows hinge + leg extension), 45° rear
- **Duration:** 45–60 sec (6–8 reps each leg)
- **Cues:** Hinge at the hip (not the waist), free leg extends directly behind, keep a neutral spine.
- **Props:** None

### 18. Split Squat
- **DB ID:** `cccc0001-0000-0000-0000-000000000032`
- **Angles:** Side profile (essential — shows stance depth), 45° front
- **Duration:** 45–60 sec (8–10 reps each leg, 3-sec lowers)
- **Cues:** Torso tall, back knee lowers straight down toward the floor, drive through the front heel. Show a slow 3-count descent.
- **Props:** Mat (optional, under the back knee)

### Seated / chair exercises

### 19. Thoracic Extension (Chair-Assisted)
- **DB ID:** `cccc0001-0000-0000-0000-000000000002`
- **Angles:** Side profile (essential — shows extension arc over chair back)
- **Duration:** 30–45 sec (5–6 reps, slow)
- **Cues:** Hands support the head. Extend the upper back over the chair back — NOT the lumbar spine.
- **Props:** Sturdy chair with a firm back edge

## Tier 2 — Bands / Dumbbells

Film in the same session as Tier 1 if possible. Add a set of loop bands and 1–2 dumbbell pairs to props.

### 20. Banded Clamshell
- **DB ID:** `cccc0001-0000-0000-0000-000000000010`
- **Angles:** Side profile facing camera (shows hip opening against band resistance)
- **Duration:** 45 sec (12–15 reps each side)
- **Cues:** Same as bodyweight clamshell — pelvis doesn't roll, controlled return.
- **Props:** Mat, light loop resistance band (above the knees)

### 21. Banded Row
- **DB ID:** `cccc0001-0000-0000-0000-000000000033`
- **Angles:** Side profile (essential — shows tall posture + row arc)
- **Duration:** 45–60 sec (10–12 reps)
- **Cues:** Anchor at chest height (or seated, band around feet). Pull to the lower ribs, squeeze the shoulder blades, release slowly.
- **Props:** Long resistance band + anchor point (door anchor or sturdy post)

### 22. Banded Romanian Deadlift
- **DB ID:** `cccc0001-0000-0000-0000-000000000012`
- **Angles:** Side profile (essential), 45° rear (shows flat back)
- **Duration:** 45–60 sec (10–12 reps)
- **Cues:** Hinge at the hips not the waist, feel hamstring tension before returning, flat back throughout.
- **Props:** Long loop resistance band (stand on it, hold at hip height)

### 23. Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift
- **DB ID:** `cccc0001-0000-0000-0000-000000000013`
- **Angles:** Side profile (essential), 45° rear
- **Duration:** 45–60 sec (8–10 reps)
- **Cues:** Same cues as banded RDL. Show a slow 3-count eccentric (lowering phase).
- **Props:** 2 dumbbells (15–25 lbs appropriate for demo)

### 24. Goblet Squat
- **DB ID:** `cccc0001-0000-0000-0000-000000000014`
- **Angles:** Side profile, 45° front
- **Duration:** 45–60 sec (8–10 reps)
- **Cues:** Goblet held at chest, elbows track inside the knees, chest tall, controlled descent.
- **Props:** 1 dumbbell (20–35 lbs appropriate)

### 25. Suitcase Carry
- **DB ID:** `cccc0001-0000-0000-0000-000000000034`
- **Angles:** Front (essential — shows level shoulders / no lean), side profile walking pass
- **Duration:** 45–60 sec (2 walking passes of ~30 sec, switch hands between passes)
- **Cues:** Walk tall, weight in one hand, opposite shoulder stays level — no leaning toward or away from the weight.
- **Props:** 1 dumbbell or kettlebell (25–40 lbs appropriate), ~5m of clear walking space

## Tier 3 — Gym Equipment

Film at a gym. Keep background clean — plain wall or equipment in soft focus. No identifiable brand logos in frame.

### 26. Barbell Hip Thrust
- **DB ID:** `cccc0001-0000-0000-0000-000000000015`
- **Angles:** Side profile (essential)
- **Duration:** 45–60 sec (8–10 reps)
- **Cues:** Upper back on bench, full hip extension at top — don't hyperextend the lumbar spine.
- **Props:** Barbell, flat bench, barbell pad (for comfort over hips)

### 27. Seated Cable Row
- **DB ID:** `cccc0001-0000-0000-0000-000000000016`
- **Angles:** Side profile (shows trunk posture + row arc)
- **Duration:** 45–60 sec (10–12 reps)
- **Cues:** Sit tall throughout. Don't lean back to pull — the row comes from the shoulder blades squeezing.
- **Props:** Cable machine, V-bar or straight bar handle

### 28. Leg Press
- **DB ID:** `cccc0001-0000-0000-0000-000000000017`
- **Angles:** Side profile (shows knee angle + range of motion)
- **Duration:** 45–60 sec (10–12 reps)
- **Cues:** Stop short of lumbar rounding at the bottom. Full foot contact on the platform, push through mid-foot.
- **Props:** Leg press machine

### 29. Kettlebell Deadlift
- **DB ID:** `cccc0001-0000-0000-0000-000000000035`
- **Angles:** Side profile (essential — shows flat back + hip drive), 45° front
- **Duration:** 45–60 sec (6–8 reps, controlled)
- **Cues:** Kettlebell between the feet. Hinge with a flat back, grip, stand tall by driving the hips forward. Lower under control — no rounding.
- **Props:** Kettlebell (16–24 kg appropriate for demo)

### 30. Back Extension (45°)
- **DB ID:** `cccc0001-0000-0000-0000-000000000036`
- **Angles:** Side profile (essential — shows hinge arc + neutral spine)
- **Duration:** 45–60 sec (8–10 reps, slow)
- **Cues:** Hips on the pad, hinge down with a neutral spine, raise until the body is one straight line — do NOT overextend past neutral.
- **Props:** 45° back extension bench

## Production Checklist

### Before shooting
- [ ] Solid neutral background (white, light gray, or cream — no patterns, no logos)
- [ ] Subject wears simple fitted clothing — solid color, NOT the same as the background
- [ ] Good even lighting (no harsh shadows, no overexposure)
- [ ] Camera mounted stable on tripod
- [ ] Shoot at 1080p/30fps minimum (4K preferred)

### Filming order (efficiency)
1. Prone floor exercise (1)
2. Supine floor exercises (2–6): stay face-up
3. Side-lying exercises (7–10)
4. All-fours exercises (11–13)
5. Standing exercises (14–18)
6. Chair exercise (19)
7. Break — switch to bands/dumbbells props
8. Band exercises (20–22), then dumbbell (23–25)
9. Separate day at gym (26–30)

### For each exercise
- [ ] Record 3–4 complete reps minimum
- [ ] Record from at least 2 angles
- [ ] Record a clean "start position" static hold (2–3 sec) before moving
- [ ] Start in position immediately — no talking head intro
- [ ] End in start position or hold (avoid cutting mid-rep)
