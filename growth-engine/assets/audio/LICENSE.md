# YouTube Short beds

40-second AAC excerpts used only under Remedy slideshow Shorts. Source MP3s stay in `src/` (gitignored) so we never ship or re-upload the full files. Shipping cuts are loudness-matched.

All tracks: Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com) — [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Commercial use and YouTube monetization are allowed. Attribution is required — the engine appends the credit to every YouTube description. Do not use these beds as standalone music uploads.

The shipping pool is the widely played set (`upbeat` in `apps/server/src/generation/short.ts`). Legacy beds stay for posts that already name them, and for meme reels.
