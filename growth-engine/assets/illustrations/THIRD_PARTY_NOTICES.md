## Remedy flat — `flat/` (owned)

Original generated scene tiles authored for Remedy, matching the app
onboarding illustrations. Soft blobs, no outlines, faceless figures,
mustard / sage / coral / teal. Not derived from Hinge Health art, Humaaans,
Open Doodles, or illlustrations.co. Commercial ads and social use are
allowed with no attribution.

## Remedy originals — `custom/` (owned)

Original vector drawings authored for Remedy, including `desk-worker`,
`phone-app`, `spine`, and the prompt-mapped set from
`scripts/build_remedy_figures.py`. Not derived from Humaaans, Open Doodles,
or illlustrations.co path data. Flat geometric figures are a general
illustration convention; the drawings themselves are original works Remedy
owns. Commercial ads and social use are allowed with no attribution.

## Sources evaluated and rejected

- Lukasz Adam (CC0) — downloads are Gumroad-gated; not automatable. Can be
  added manually later.
- unDraw / ManyPixels — commercial use OK but license prohibits bulk
  pack redistribution; not vendored.
- Storyset, Streamline free, IconScout free — attribution required on ads.
- GetIllustrations free — personal use only, excludes marketing/social.
- CtrlV.design — claims CC0 but provenance is too weak to rely on.
- SVG Repo — per-asset licensing with CC-BY-NC traps.
