Exercises for upper and middle back pain

A tight upper back usually responds well to two things: moving it more, especially into extension and rotation, and making the muscles between your shoulder blades stronger.

Key takeaways

  • Most upper back stiffness comes with long periods in one position, often sitting at a screen.
  • Combine thoracic mobility (rotation and extension) with rows that strengthen the muscles around your shoulder blades.
  • Upper back pain with chest pain, shortness of breath, or fever needs medical care, not exercise.

Why does the upper back get tight?

The upper and middle back, called the thoracic spine, is built for rotation and some extension. Long stretches of sitting and looking down at a screen keep it rounded and still for hours. For most people the result is stiffness and an ache between the shoulder blades, not an injury. Most back pain, upper or lower, improves with time and staying active 1.

Mobility: get it moving

  • Open-book rotation: side-lying rotation, 8 per side. Keep your knees stacked so the twist comes from your upper back.
  • Chair thoracic extension: lean back over a chair, 8 reps. Easy to do at a desk.
  • Cat-cow stretch: moves the whole spine, upper back included.

Strength: build the muscles that hold you up

Mobility makes the upper back feel better. Strength makes it easier to sit and stand tall without effort. Rows are the main tool:

  • Resistance band row: pull a band to your lower ribs and squeeze your shoulder blades. 3 sets of 12.
  • Seated cable row: the gym version, with load you can increase in small steps.

Remedy’s upper back sessions pair these: a mobility warm-up, rows for strength, and a cooldown.

Fitting it into a workday

  • Change position at least every 30 to 60 minutes. Stand up, walk to get water, look away from the screen.
  • Do a few chair thoracic extensions between meetings. They take under a minute.
  • Save rows and the fuller routine for a short session before or after work.

The guide for people who sit all day covers the low back side of desk work too.

When upper back pain needs a doctor

Upper back pain can sometimes come from something other than muscles and joints. Get urgent medical help if it comes with chest pain, shortness of breath, sweating, or nausea, or if you have a fever or feel unwell 2. Pain after a fall or injury also needs checking. The full list of warning signs is in its own guide.

Questions

What causes upper back pain from sitting?

Usually spending a long time in one position, often rounded toward a screen, rather than any damage. Moving more often and strengthening the upper back tend to help.

What is the best exercise for upper back pain?

A combination works best: a rotation like the open-book, an extension like the chair thoracic extension, and a row to build the muscles between your shoulder blades.

When is upper back pain serious?

Get urgent medical help if upper back pain comes with chest pain, shortness of breath, sweating, fever, or after an injury.

When to see someone instead of using an app

Get medical care, not an exercise routine, if you have back pain with any of these:

  • Numbness or tingling around your genitals or buttocks
  • Trouble peeing, or loss of bladder or bowel control
  • Numbness, tingling, or weakness in one or both legs that is getting worse
  • Back pain after a fall, car accident, or other injury
  • A fever, or feeling unwell, along with back pain
  • Unexplained weight loss, or a history of cancer
  • Pain that is severe, constant, and gets worse at night or when lying down
  • Pain that keeps getting worse over several weeks instead of better
