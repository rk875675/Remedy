Questions

More questions

What is Remedy?

Remedy is an iPhone app for people with back pain. You answer a few questions. It builds a 10 to 16 week program of short sessions, with a video on every exercise. It is a fitness program, not medical care.

How much does Remedy cost?

Free to download. Full access is $4.99 a week, $12.99 a month, or $79.99 a year (about $6.67 a month). Trials, where offered, are 7 days on weekly and yearly plans and 14 days on monthly. You pay in the App Store. Pricing.

Is Remedy physical therapy?

No. Remedy does not diagnose or treat. It is not a substitute for a doctor or a physical therapist.

What does the armadillo do?

You name it. It starts curled up, at 0 finished sessions. It moves up at 1, 4, 9, and 15 finished sessions. Missed days do not shrink it. It does not describe your back. More about the armadillo.

Is there an Android app?

No. Remedy is iPhone only. It needs iOS 15.1 or later.
