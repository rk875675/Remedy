FAQ

Quick questions

What kind of back pain app is actually useful?

One that gives you a plan, not a pile of stretches to pick from. You answer a few questions, it builds a multi-week program, and every exercise has a video. That's Remedy.

How do you work on a sore back with an app?

Don't treat it like YouTube. Open the app, do today's session, check in on how you feel, done. Short sessions you repeat beat one long video you never finish.

Who is Remedy for?

Anyone with a sore back who wants a real program — desk people, parents, gym-goers who tweaked something. If something feels acute or scary, see a person first, not an app.
