## Every plan includes

- A 10 to 16 week program built from your answers

- A video on every exercise

- Sessions of 15, 20, or 30 minutes, on 3 to 7 days you pick

- A pain rating before and after each session

- A new program, or a harder one, when you finish

- An armadillo that moves up at 1, 4, 9, and 15 finished sessions. Missed days do not shrink it.

## Billing, cancelling, and refunds

### Is Remedy free?

Remedy is free to download. Full access to your program needs a subscription.

### Is there a free trial?

Where offered, the weekly and annual plans include a 7-day free trial and the monthly plan includes a 14-day free trial. A trial converts to a paid subscription unless you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends.

### How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and cancel Remedy there. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

### Can I get a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple. You can request one at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/).

### Do prices differ outside the US?

Yes. These are US prices. The App Store shows the price in your local currency before you subscribe.

### Is Remedy covered by insurance or my employer?

No. Remedy is a consumer app you subscribe to yourself. It is not an employer benefit or a covered medical service.

Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase. The subscription renews unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. See the [Terms of Service](https://remedyrecoveries.com/terms).
