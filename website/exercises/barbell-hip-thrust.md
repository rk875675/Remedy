# Barbell hip thrust

A loaded bridge with your upper back on a bench and a barbell across your hips. It lets you load the glutes heavily without putting the weight on your spine.

Also called: hip thrust, weighted hip thrust

- **Type:** Glutes and hips

- **Equipment:** Gym

- **Area:** Lower back

- **Level:** Advanced

- **Starting dose in Remedy:** 3 sets of 10, last two reps hard

## How to do it

- Sit on the floor with your upper back against the side of a bench and a padded barbell over your hips.
- Bend your knees and plant your feet flat, about hip-width apart.
- Drive through your heels to lift your hips until they lock out and your shins are vertical.
- Keep your chin tucked and ribs down at the top, then lower under control.

## Form cues

- Drive the hips up until they lock out.
- Don’t arch the low back.
- The last two reps should feel hard.

## Common mistakes

- Finishing the rep by arching the low back instead of extending the hips.
- Feet too far forward, which shifts the work to the hamstrings.

## Easier and harder versions

- **Easier:** [Single-leg glute bridge](https://remedyrecoveries.com/exercises/single-leg-glute-bridge)

## Why it helps a sore back

For people who train in a gym, it is a way to keep building hip strength with real load while the low back stays out of the heavy lifting.
