# Side plank from the knees

A shorter-lever side plank with your knees on the floor. It builds endurance in the muscles on the side of the trunk.

Also called: modified side plank, kneeling side plank

- **Type:** Core and stability

- **Equipment:** No equipment

- **Area:** Lower back

- **Level:** Intermediate

- **Starting dose in Remedy:** 3 sets of 4 holds of 10 seconds per side

## How to do it

- Lie on your side with your elbow under your shoulder and your knees bent behind you.
- Stack your hips and shoulders.
- Lift your hips until your ear, shoulder, hip, and knee form a straight line.
- Hold for about ten seconds, breathing normally, then lower.
- Do your holds, then switch sides.

## Form cues

- Lift the hips so ear, shoulder, hip, and knee line up.
- Don’t let the hips sag.

## Common mistakes

- Letting the top hip roll forward or back.
- Shrugging the shoulder up toward the ear. Push the floor away.

## Easier and harder versions

- **Easier:** [Bird dog exercise](https://remedyrecoveries.com/exercises/bird-dog)

- **Harder:** [Side plank](https://remedyrecoveries.com/exercises/side-plank)

## Why it helps a sore back

The side of the trunk keeps you upright when you carry something in one hand or walk on uneven ground. Starting from the knees lets you build that endurance before taking the full plank.
