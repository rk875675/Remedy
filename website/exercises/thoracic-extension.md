# Chair thoracic extension

A gentle backward bend of the upper back over the top of a chair. You can do it at a desk in under a minute.

Also called: seated thoracic extension, upper back extension over chair

- **Type:** Mobility

- **Equipment:** No equipment

- **Area:** Upper and mid back

- **Level:** Gentle

- **Starting dose in Remedy:** 2 sets of 8

## How to do it

- Sit tall in a chair with a firm back that reaches about the bottom of your shoulder blades.
- Put your hands behind your head and bring your elbows in a little.
- Lean back over the top edge of the chair, letting your upper back extend.
- Pause for a breath, then return to sitting tall.

## Form cues

- Hands behind your head.
- Arch only the upper back over the chair, not the low back.

## Common mistakes

- Pushing the ribs and belly forward, which turns it into a low back arch.
- Pulling on the head with the hands.

## Easier and harder versions

- Remedy keeps this one steady and changes the work around it instead.

## Why it helps a sore back

Long stretches of sitting and looking at a screen keep the upper back rounded. This moves it the other way, using the chair back as a pivot so the bend comes from the upper back and not the lower back.
