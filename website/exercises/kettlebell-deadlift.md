# Kettlebell deadlift

Lifting a kettlebell from the floor between your feet using a hip hinge. It is how you learn to pick something heavy up off the ground.

Also called: KB deadlift, kettlebell deadlift form

- **Type:** Strength

- **Equipment:** Gym

- **Area:** Lower back

- **Level:** Advanced

- **Starting dose in Remedy:** 3 sets of 8, last two reps hard

## How to do it

- Stand with the kettlebell on the floor between your feet, in line with your heels or mid-foot.
- Hinge your hips back and bend your knees enough to grip the handle with a flat back.
- Brace, then stand up by driving your hips forward until you are tall.
- Hinge back down the same way and set the bell down with control.

## Form cues

- Hinge, grip, stand by driving the hips forward.
- Keep a flat back.
- The last two reps should feel hard.

## Common mistakes

- Squatting down with the chest dropping, or rounding over to reach the bell.
- Leaning back at the top.

## Easier and harder versions

- **Easier:** [Dumbbell Romanian deadlift](https://remedyrecoveries.com/exercises/dumbbell-romanian-deadlift) or [Hip hinge](https://remedyrecoveries.com/exercises/bodyweight-hip-hinge)

## Why it helps a sore back

Picking things up off the floor is one of the moments people worry about most with a sore back. Practicing it with a manageable load builds confidence and strength for it.
