# Banded Romanian deadlift

A hip hinge against a resistance band anchored under your feet. The band adds load at the top of the movement without heavy weight.

Also called: resistance band RDL, band deadlift

- **Type:** Strength

- **Equipment:** Band or dumbbells

- **Area:** Lower back

- **Level:** Intermediate

- **Starting dose in Remedy:** 3 sets of 12, last two reps hard

## How to do it

- Stand on the middle of a resistance band with your feet hip-width apart, holding the ends.
- Stand tall with soft knees.
- Hinge at the hips, sliding your hands down your thighs while your back stays long.
- When you feel your hamstrings, drive your hips forward to stand against the band.

## Form cues

- Hinge at the hips, not the waist.
- Feel the hamstrings, then stand.
- The last two reps should feel hard.

## Common mistakes

- Rounding the back to reach lower.
- Leaning back at the top.
