# Bird dog exercise

From your hands and knees, you reach one arm forward and the opposite leg back while keeping your back still. It is one of the most widely used exercises for back stability.

Also called: bird dogs, quadruped opposite arm leg raise

- **Type:** Core and stability

- **Equipment:** No equipment

- **Area:** Lower back

- **Level:** Beginner

- **Starting dose in Remedy:** 3 sets of 5 per side, holding each rep for 10 seconds

## How to do it

- Start on your hands and knees, hands under shoulders and knees under hips, with a flat back.
- Gently brace your stomach.
- Slide one leg back and lift it to hip height while reaching the opposite arm forward to shoulder height.
- Hold for about ten seconds, keeping your hips level and your back still.
- Lower with control and switch sides.

## Form cues

- Reach opposite arm and leg.
- Keep the back still and the hips level.

## Common mistakes

- Lifting the leg too high, which arches the low back.
- Rotating the hips open. Imagine balancing a cup of water on your lower back.
- Rushing between sides.

## Easier and harder versions

- **Easier:** [Dead bug exercise](https://remedyrecoveries.com/exercises/dead-bug)

- **To make it easier:** Move only the arms first, then only the legs, before combining them.

- **Harder:** [Side plank from the knees](https://remedyrecoveries.com/exercises/side-plank-knees)

## Why it helps a sore back

It trains the muscles along the spine and around the trunk to keep the back steady while the hips and shoulders move. Remedy uses short holds of about ten seconds, repeated, which tend to work better than one long shaky hold.
