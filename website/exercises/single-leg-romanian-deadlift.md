# Single-leg Romanian deadlift

A hip hinge on one leg while the other leg reaches straight back. It builds strength in the back of the hips and legs and challenges balance.

Also called: single leg RDL, single leg deadlift bodyweight

- **Type:** Strength

- **Equipment:** No equipment

- **Area:** Lower back

- **Level:** Intermediate

- **Starting dose in Remedy:** 3 sets of 8 per side

## How to do it

- Stand on one leg with a soft knee. Hold a wall or chair for balance if you need it.
- Hinge forward at the hip while the free leg reaches straight back.
- Keep your back long and your hips square to the floor.
- When you feel the hamstring of the standing leg, drive back up to standing.
- Finish your reps and switch legs.

## Form cues

- Hinge at the hip, not the waist.
- The free leg reaches straight back; keep the back long.

## Common mistakes

- Opening the hip of the free leg toward the ceiling.
- Rounding the back to get the hands lower.

## Why it helps a sore back

It loads one hip at a time with no equipment, which makes it a useful step up for people training at home.
