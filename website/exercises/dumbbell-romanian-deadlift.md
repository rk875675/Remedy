# Dumbbell Romanian deadlift

A hip hinge holding a dumbbell in each hand. A slow lower builds strength through the hamstrings, glutes, and the muscles along the spine.

Also called: dumbbell RDL, DB RDL

- **Type:** Strength

- **Equipment:** Band or dumbbells

- **Area:** Lower back

- **Level:** Advanced

- **Starting dose in Remedy:** 3 sets of 10, lowering over 3 seconds

## How to do it

- Stand tall holding a dumbbell in each hand in front of your thighs.
- Soften your knees and hinge at the hips, sliding the weights down close to your legs.
- Take about three seconds to lower, keeping your back long.
- When you feel a strong hamstring stretch, usually around mid-shin, drive your hips forward to stand.

## Form cues

- Hinge with a long back.
- Lower for 3 seconds, then stand.
- The last two reps should feel hard.

## Common mistakes

- Letting the dumbbells drift away from your legs.
- Rounding the back at the bottom to go lower.
