# Seated cable row

A seated horizontal pull on a cable machine. It builds the muscles between and around the shoulder blades.

Also called: cable row, low row machine

- **Type:** Strength

- **Equipment:** Gym

- **Area:** Upper and mid back

- **Level:** Intermediate

- **Starting dose in Remedy:** 3 sets of 12, last two reps hard

## How to do it

- Sit at the cable row station with your feet on the platform and knees slightly bent.
- Grip the handle and sit tall with your arms straight.
- Pull the handle to your stomach by squeezing your shoulder blades together, keeping your torso still.
- Let the weight back out slowly until your arms are straight.

## Form cues

- Sit tall.
- Pull by squeezing the shoulder blades; don’t lean back.
- The last two reps should feel hard.

## Common mistakes

- Rocking the torso back and forth to move the weight.
- Rounding forward at the end of each rep.

## Easier and harder versions

- **Easier:** [Resistance band row](https://remedyrecoveries.com/exercises/banded-row)

## Why it helps a sore back

It is the gym version of the band row, with load you can increase in small steps.
