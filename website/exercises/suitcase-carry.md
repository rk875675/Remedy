# Suitcase carry

Walking while holding a weight in one hand, like carrying a suitcase. The goal is to stay upright and not lean.

Also called: single-arm farmer carry, one-arm farmers walk

- **Type:** Core and stability

- **Equipment:** Band or dumbbells

- **Area:** Lower back

- **Level:** Advanced

- **Starting dose in Remedy:** 3 carries of 30 seconds per side

## How to do it

- Pick up a dumbbell or kettlebell in one hand using a hip hinge, not by rounding your back.
- Stand tall with your shoulders level and the weight by your side.
- Walk at a normal pace for about 30 seconds, keeping your trunk upright.
- Set the weight down with the same hinge, switch hands, and repeat.

## Form cues

- Walk tall.
- Keep both shoulders level; don’t lean toward or away from the weight.

## Common mistakes

- Leaning away from the weight to balance it.
- Shrugging the loaded shoulder up.
- Starting too heavy. Choose a weight you can carry with good posture the whole time.

## Easier and harder versions

- **Easier:** [Side plank](https://remedyrecoveries.com/exercises/side-plank)

## Why it helps a sore back

Carrying something on one side tries to bend you sideways. Resisting that is exactly what the side-of-trunk muscles do in daily life, from grocery bags to a child on one hip.

In the app it is called **Suitcase Carry**. Remedy is more likely to pick it if you say your back is bothered by standing or walking or working out.
