# Figure-4 stretch

A stretch for the glutes and the piriformis, done lying on your back with one ankle crossed over the opposite knee.

Also called: figure four stretch, supine piriformis stretch, glute stretch lying down

- **Type:** Stretches and recovery

- **Equipment:** No equipment

- **Area:** Lower back

- **Level:** Gentle

- **Starting dose in Remedy:** 2 holds of 30 seconds per side

## How to do it

- Lie on your back with both knees bent and feet flat.
- Cross one ankle over the opposite knee.
- Reach through and hold behind the thigh of the uncrossed leg.
- Gently pull that thigh toward your chest until you feel a stretch in the buttock of the crossed leg.
- Hold, then switch sides.

## Form cues

- Cross ankle over opposite knee.
- Gently pull the uncrossed thigh toward you.

## Common mistakes

- Lifting the head and shoulders off the floor. Use a strap or towel if you cannot reach.

## Easier and harder versions

- Remedy keeps this one steady and changes the work around it instead.

## Why it helps a sore back

Tight glutes are a common complaint alongside low back stiffness, and this stretch reaches them without loading the back.
