# Child’s pose for back pain

A resting stretch where you sit back toward your heels with your arms stretched out in front. It eases the low back and hips at the end of a session.

Also called: child pose stretch, balasana

- **Type:** Stretches and recovery

- **Equipment:** No equipment

- **Area:** Lower back

- **Level:** Gentle

- **Starting dose in Remedy:** 2 holds of 40 seconds

## How to do it

- Kneel with your big toes together and your knees apart.
- Sit your hips back toward your heels.
- Walk your hands forward and rest your forehead on the floor or a cushion.
- Breathe slowly into your back and sides and hold.

## Form cues

- Sit back toward your heels, arms long, forehead down.
- Breathe into the stretch.

## Common mistakes

- Forcing the hips down to the heels. Go as far as is comfortable.

## Easier and harder versions

- **To make it easier:** Put a pillow between your heels and hips, or under your chest, if you cannot sit all the way back.

## Why it helps a sore back

It is an easy, supported position to finish a session in, and a common way to wind down the low back after strength work.
