# Glute bridge

Lying on your back with knees bent, you drive through your heels to lift your hips. It strengthens the glutes, the main muscles that extend the hips.

Also called: hip bridge, bridge exercise

- **Type:** Glutes and hips

- **Equipment:** No equipment

- **Area:** Lower back

- **Level:** Beginner

- **Starting dose in Remedy:** 3 sets of 12

## How to do it

- Lie on your back with your knees bent and feet flat, about hip-width apart.
- Rest your arms by your sides.
- Push through your heels and squeeze your glutes to lift your hips until your shoulders, hips, and knees line up.
- Pause at the top, then lower slowly.

## Form cues

- Drive through your heels and squeeze.
- Keep the ribs down; don’t arch the low back.

## Common mistakes

- Pushing the hips too high and arching the low back.
- Feeling it mostly in the hamstrings. Bring your feet a little closer to your hips.

## Easier and harder versions

- **Harder:** [Single-leg glute bridge](https://remedyrecoveries.com/exercises/single-leg-glute-bridge)

## Why it helps a sore back

Strong glutes take load off the low back when you lift, stand up, or climb stairs. The bridge is a simple, well-supported way to start building them.
