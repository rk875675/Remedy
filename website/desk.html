Back pain from sitting all day

If your back gets tight from a chair and a screen, the fix is less about perfect posture and more about moving often and building a back that handles long days.

Key takeaways

  • There is no single perfect sitting posture. Staying in any one position for hours is the bigger issue.
  • Change position every 30 to 60 minutes, and add a short routine a few times a week.
  • Hip flexor, upper back, and glute work target the areas long sitting affects most.

Why does sitting all day make my back hurt?

Desk stiffness is one of the most common reasons people look for help with their back. For most people, the problem is not that sitting damages the spine. Most back pain has no specific structural cause 1. It is that the back stays in one position for hours. Hip flexors stay shortened, the upper back rounds toward the screen, and the glutes barely work.

Is there a perfect posture?

No. Sitting up straight is fine, and so is leaning back, as long as you don’t stay frozen in any of them. A good chair and screen height make long days more comfortable, but they don’t replace moving. The best posture is the next one.

What actually helps

  • Move often. Get up every 30 to 60 minutes: refill water, take a call standing, walk to a colleague.
  • Stay active overall. Guidelines recommend staying active and exercising for low back pain 23.
  • Build the muscles sitting switches off. Glutes, upper back, and the muscles that keep your trunk steady.

A short routine for between meetings

  1. Chair thoracic extension: 8 reps over the back of your chair.
  2. Hip flexor stretch: about 30 seconds per side.
  3. Glute bridge: 12 reps on the floor if you have space.
  4. Open-book rotation: 8 per side.

If you have a resistance band at your desk, a set of resistance band row reps is a good addition for the upper back. Upper back pain exercises covers that side in more detail.

Sitting and the next morning

Long days in a chair often show up the next morning too. Why your lower back is stiff in the morning covers that side. If you want to start on your own, a no-equipment back routine fits next to a desk, and how long to stick with it sets realistic expectations.

Questions

Why does my back hurt after sitting all day?

Usually because it has stayed in one position for hours, not because sitting is damaging it. Moving more often and building strength in the hips and back tend to help.

What is the best sitting posture for back pain?

There isn’t one perfect posture. The best posture is the next one: change position regularly, stand up, and move.

Do standing desks help back pain?

They can help by making it easier to change position, but standing all day is not the goal either. Alternating between sitting, standing, and moving is what matters.

When to see someone instead of using an app

Get medical care, not an exercise routine, if you have back pain with any of these:

  • Numbness or tingling around your genitals or buttocks
  • Trouble peeing, or loss of bladder or bowel control
  • Numbness, tingling, or weakness in one or both legs that is getting worse
  • Back pain after a fall, car accident, or other injury
  • A fever, or feeling unwell, along with back pain
  • Unexplained weight loss, or a history of cancer
  • Pain that is severe, constant, and gets worse at night or when lying down
  • Pain that keeps getting worse over several weeks instead of better
