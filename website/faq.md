## The app

### What is Remedy?

Remedy: Back Pain Relief is an iPhone app for people with back pain. You answer a short onboarding about your back, and it builds a multi-week back fitness program of short, video-guided sessions with before-and-after pain check-ins.

### Who is Remedy for?

Adults 17 and older with everyday back pain who want a structured plan: desk workers, parents, people who lift, and people who just want to move more comfortably. It is not for people with warning signs or a new injury.

### Is it for lower back or upper back pain?

Both. Onboarding asks whether it is your upper back, lower back, or all over, and the program adapts.

### How long is a program?

10 to 16 weeks, depending on how long your back has been bothering you and how it behaves. When it ends you can build a new program or level up your current one.

### How long is each session?

You choose 15, 20, or 30 minutes. Each session shows its length and its exercises before you start.

### Do I need equipment?

No. You choose full gym access, bands and light dumbbells, or open floor space, and the program uses only what you have.

### Is Remedy on Android?

No. Remedy is iPhone only. It needs iOS 15.1 or later.

## Safety and evidence

### Is Remedy physical therapy?

No. Remedy is a fitness app. It does not diagnose or treat conditions and is not a substitute for a doctor or physical therapist.

### Is Remedy safe to use with back pain?

For everyday back pain in people with no warning signs, exercise is one of the most recommended options in clinical guidelines. Stop any exercise that causes sharp pain or pain spreading down a leg, and see a clinician if you have [warning signs](https://remedyrecoveries.com/guides/when-to-see-a-doctor-for-back-pain).

### Is Remedy backed by research?

The approach, a progressive program of mobility, stability, and strength exercise, follows what guidelines like NICE and the American College of Physicians recommend for low back pain. Remedy has not run its own clinical trial of the app. Our guides link to every source.

### Who writes the guides?

The Remedy team. Guides are general fitness information based on published clinical guidelines and research, and every claim links to its source. They are not written or reviewed by a clinician. See our [editorial standards](https://remedyrecoveries.com/about#editorial).

## Your armadillo

### What is the armadillo?

Every Remedy account comes with one armadillo that you name. It shows how many sessions you have finished. It is not a comment on your back, and it is not a promise about pain.

### When does the armadillo move up?

It has five stages. Curled is 0 finished sessions. It moves up at 1, 4, 9, and 15 finished sessions, the 1st, 4th, 9th, and 15th. Missed days do not shrink it. It does not describe your back.

### What does the armadillo do all day?

It stays at the stage you have reached. It does not describe your back.
