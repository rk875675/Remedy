# How Remedy works, step by step

A few questions become a program. Then you get one session at a time.

A fitness program, not medical care.

1

## Answer a few questions about your back

This takes a few minutes. A sample of the questions:

- **Where does it hurt?:** Upper back, lower back, or all over

- **How active are you?:** Mostly sitting, lightly active, or strength training

- **What makes it worse?:** One example: mornings, or after you have been resting

2

## Get a program from those answers

- **Length.** 10 to 16 weeks. It depends on how long your back has bothered you, and how it behaves.

- **Days.** You pick 3 to 7 days a week.

- **Exercises.** They follow where it hurts, what makes it worse, and the equipment you have.

- **Progress.** The work gets harder across the weeks.

The rules come from your answers. A therapist is not reviewing your case. The camera does not score your form.

3

## Do the session for today

You choose 15, 20, or 30 minutes. Before you start, the session shows the time, the focus, and the equipment.

- Rate your pain before you start, and again when you finish.

- Warm up.

- Do the main work. Each exercise has a video, sets, reps, and one cue.

- Cool down.

- Rate your pain again.

The armadillo is on the session screen. It moves up at 1, 4, 9, and 15 finished sessions. It does not describe your back. Missed days do not shrink it.

4

## Watch the trend, not one day

Progress shows how far you are in the program, how many sessions you finished this week, and your pain over time.

A hard day can still happen. The trend is the part to read.

5

## When the program ends

**Build my next program** asks a short check-in and writes a new plan. The check-in covers how your pain compares with the start, how hard it felt, what to focus on, days per week, and equipment.

**Level up my current plan** keeps the same plan, one level harder. A report of worse pain does not raise the level.

## What Remedy does not do

Remedy does not diagnose a cause, treat a condition, or replace a physician or a physical therapist. The videos show the movements. They are not medical care. If you have [warning signs](https://remedyrecoveries.com/guides/when-to-see-a-doctor-for-back-pain), stop and get medical care.

There is no live coach, no community feed, and no Android app. Remedy is for the back only.
