# Back Pain Made*Less Boring*

An iPhone program of short sessions, with a video on every move. Free to download. A fitness program, not medical care.

[](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/remedy-back-pain-relief/id6813745106)

## The program

### Your week, mapped out

10 to 16 weeks. 15, 20, or 30 minutes a session.

### Your days. Your gear.

Pick 3 to 7 days. Full gym, bands and light dumbbells, or just the floor.

## Today’s session is already chosen

Video on every exercise, with sets, reps, and one form cue. You rate pain 0 to 10 before you start and when you finish.

## Questions

[More questions](https://remedyrecoveries.com/faq)

### What is Remedy?

Remedy is an iPhone app for people with back pain. You answer a few questions. It builds a 10 to 16 week program of short sessions, with a video on every exercise. It is a fitness program, not medical care.

### How much does Remedy cost?

Free to download. Full access is $4.99 a week, $12.99 a month, or $79.99 a year (about $6.67 a month). Trials, where offered, are 7 days on weekly and yearly plans and 14 days on monthly. You pay in the App Store. [Pricing](https://remedyrecoveries.com/pricing).

### Is Remedy physical therapy?

No. Remedy does not diagnose or treat. It is not a substitute for a doctor or a physical therapist.

### What does the armadillo do?

You name it. It starts curled up, at 0 finished sessions. It moves up at 1, 4, 9, and 15 finished sessions. Missed days do not shrink it. It does not describe your back. [More about the armadillo](https://remedyrecoveries.com/faq#armadillo).

### Is there an Android app?

No. Remedy is iPhone only. It needs iOS 15.1 or later.
