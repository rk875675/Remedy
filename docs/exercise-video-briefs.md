# Exercise video briefs

Source spec for the clips of the exercises added in migration 093 and held back in 094. Pallof Press is out of the program. 90-90 Breathing gets a breathing screen instead of a video and is described last, for reference only.

## Rules for every clip

- **One person, whole body visible in every frame.** Head, hands and feet stay in frame for the whole clip, including the furthest point of the movement. Nothing cut off at the edges.
- **Clear view.** Plain, uncluttered background, even light, fitted clothing that contrasts with the background, so the spine, hips and knees are easy to read. No other people, no text overlays, no logos, no watermarks, no music cues needed.
- **Camera still.** Locked off, no zoom or pan. Camera angle as listed per exercise.
- **Clip length.** Starts in the start position, already still. Shows 1–2 clean reps (or, for a per-side move, 1–2 reps on one side). Ends back in the start position, still. Trim everything before the start position and after the return: no walking into frame, no setup fiddling, no talking to camera, no rest.
- **Landscape.** The player crops to 20:13 (`pt-video-crop-center` skill), so leave space around the person on all sides.
- **Correct form only.** If a source clip shows any of the listed mistakes, do not use it.
- **Equipment.** Only what is listed: floor or mat, a sturdy chair, a wall, a single stair or low step, a long resistance band, a mini loop band. Nothing else.

Pacing is slow and controlled throughout. These are rehab movements, not workouts.

## 1. Double Knee-to-Chest
Pattern: lumbar flexion · Floor · Both sides at once

Replaces Pelvic Tilt. Same goal, a flexion of the low back, with a movement large enough to see.

**Camera:** three-quarter view along the body, head at one side of the frame and feet at the other. Both knees stay visible. Not overhead. Not a flat side-on profile.

**Start position:** lying on the back on a mat. Both knees bent, feet flat, head resting on the mat.

**Movement:**
1. Draw both knees toward the chest with the hands, until the knees are close to the chest and the low back rounds into the mat.
2. Hold briefly.
3. Lower both feet back to the mat.

**Correct form:** both knees travel together. Head and shoulders stay down. The pull is gentle.

**Wrong (reject the clip):** one knee only, a sit-up, a bridge, the head lifting off the mat.

**Clip:** knees bent and still → both knees to the chest → feet back down, still.

## 2. Knee-to-Chest
Pattern: lumbar flexion · Floor · Single-leg version, one side shown

**Camera:** side view at floor height, from the side of the leg being pulled.

**Start position:** lying on the back, both knees bent, feet flat on the floor, head resting on the mat.

**Movement:**
1. Lift one foot and bring that knee toward the chest.
2. Hold behind the thigh, just below the knee, with both hands, and draw the knee gently closer until a mild stretch is felt in the low back and buttock.
3. Hold, breathing slowly (show about 3–5 seconds of the hold).
4. Let go and lower that foot back to the floor.

**Correct form:** the other foot stays flat on the floor, knee bent. Head and shoulders stay on the mat. Pull is gentle, low back rounds softly into the floor.

**Wrong:** pulling on the shin with the knee fully bent and forced, lifting the head toward the knee, the other leg lifting or straightening off the floor, jerking.

**Clip:** start position → one knee in, hold → foot back down → still.

## 3. Seated Sciatic Nerve Slider
Pattern: nerve glide · Sturdy chair, no arms preferred · One side shown

**Camera:** side view at seat height, from the side of the moving leg, whole body including head and the foot.

**Start position:** sitting tall near the front edge of a sturdy chair, feet flat on the floor, hands resting on the thighs or the seat edge. Slight slump in the upper back is fine; it is not a held posture.

**Movement (the "slider": head and leg move in opposite directions):**
1. Straighten one knee, lifting the foot toward knee height, while pulling the toes up toward the shin **and at the same time** tipping the head back to look up.
2. Bend that knee back down, pointing the toes away, **while at the same time** bringing the chin down toward the chest.
3. Repeat as a smooth, continuous, gentle back-and-forth.

**Correct form:** each movement is easy, smooth and coordinated; the leg never locks hard and the head never cranks. No pain or tingling is chased.

**Wrong:** head and leg moving the same direction (chin down while the leg straightens — that is a tensioner, not a slider), kicking or locking the knee, leaning far back, holding at the end, slumping hard on purpose.

**Clip:** seated still → 2 full back-and-forth cycles → foot back on floor, still.

## 4. Side-Lying Hip Abduction
Pattern: hip abduction · Floor · One side shown

**Camera:** from the front of the person (facing their stomach), at floor height, so the top leg's path up and the hip stacking are both visible. Whole body from head to feet.

**Start position:** lying on one side in a straight line from head to heels. Bottom knee may be bent slightly for balance. Head rests on the lower arm, top hand on the floor in front of the chest. Hips stacked one directly over the other.

**Movement:**
1. Keeping the top leg straight and the toes pointing forward (not up), lift the top leg about 30–45° toward the ceiling.
2. Pause briefly at the top.
3. Lower slowly back to resting on the bottom leg.

**Correct form:** the leg moves slightly behind the body line rather than forward. The top hip stays stacked and does not roll back. Effort is felt on the side of the top hip.

**Wrong:** toes turning to the ceiling, the leg swinging forward, the top hip rolling backward, lifting very high by tilting the trunk, swinging fast.

**Clip:** start position still → 2 lifts → leg resting, still.

## 5. Banded Lateral Walk
Pattern: hip abduction · Mini loop band · Both legs

**Camera:** straight-on front view, full body, wide enough for the person to step sideways within frame.

**Start position:** standing with a mini loop band just above the knees (or around the ankles). Feet hip-width apart so the band has light tension. Knees and hips slightly bent in a quarter squat, chest up, hands on hips or clasped in front.

**Movement:**
1. Step sideways with one foot about shoulder-width, keeping tension in the band.
2. Follow with the other foot, stopping at hip-width so the band never goes slack.
3. Take 2–3 steps one way, then 2–3 steps back to where they started.

**Correct form:** the quarter-squat height stays the same throughout. Toes point forward. Knees stay pushed out over the toes against the band. The trunk stays upright and level, no rocking.

**Wrong:** knees caving in, feet coming fully together (band goes slack), standing up straight between steps, swinging the trunk side to side, toes turning out.

**Clip:** start position still → a few steps out → the same number back → still in the start position. The person ends in the same spot they started.

## 6. Plank Shoulder Taps
Pattern: anti-rotation · Floor · Alternating sides

**Camera:** side-front three-quarter view at floor height, so you can see both that the hips stay level and the hand touching the opposite shoulder.

**Start position:** high plank — hands under the shoulders, arms straight, body in a straight line from head to heels. Feet set wider than hip-width for stability.

**Movement:**
1. Without rotating the hips, lift one hand and tap the opposite shoulder.
2. Place that hand back down under its shoulder.
3. Repeat with the other hand.

**Correct form:** hips stay level and do not sway side to side. Body stays in a straight line, low back does not sag. Movement is slow.

**Wrong:** hips rocking or twisting with each tap, hips piked high, low back sagging, feet together, fast slapping taps.

**Clip:** plank still → tap left, tap right (2 taps per side max) → still in plank. Optionally end by lowering the knees to the floor — keep the person fully visible.

## 8. Wall Angel
Pattern: scapular · A wall · Both arms

**Camera:** straight-on front view facing the person, full body.

**Start position:** standing with the back, buttocks and head against a wall, feet a small step away from it. Arms raised in a "goal post": upper arms out to the sides at shoulder height, elbows bent to 90°, backs of the forearms and hands touching the wall as far as comfortable.

**Movement:**
1. Slide the arms up the wall, straightening toward overhead, keeping the arms and hands in contact with the wall as much as possible.
2. Stop where contact would be lost or the low back would arch.
3. Slide back down to the goal post, drawing the shoulder blades down and together.

**Correct form:** head, upper back and buttocks stay on the wall. Low back stays close to the wall, ribs down. Shoulders stay away from the ears. Smooth, slow.

**Wrong:** low back arching off the wall, ribs flaring, head pushing forward off the wall, shrugging, arms coming off the wall entirely, fast pumping.

**Clip:** goal post still → 2 slides up and down → goal post, still.

## 9. Band Pull-Apart
Pattern: scapular · Long resistance band (or a light flat band) · Both arms

**Camera:** front view or slight front three-quarter, full body, wide enough for the band at full width.

**Start position:** standing tall, feet hip-width. Arms straight out in front at shoulder height, palms facing down, hands holding a band about shoulder-width apart with light tension.

**Movement:**
1. Pull the band apart by moving the hands out to the sides, arms straight, until the band touches or nears the chest and the shoulder blades squeeze together.
2. Pause briefly.
3. Return the hands slowly to the front with control.

**Correct form:** arms stay at shoulder height and nearly straight. The movement comes from the shoulder blades. Neck relaxed, trunk still.

**Wrong:** shrugging, arching the low back or leaning back, bending the elbows into a row, arms dropping, letting the band snap back.

**Clip:** start position still → 2 pull-aparts → hands back in front, still.

## 10. Sit-to-Stand
Pattern: functional · Sturdy chair without wheels · Both legs

**Camera:** side view at hip height, full body including the whole chair.

**Start position:** sitting near the front half of the chair seat, feet flat hip-width apart, a little behind the knees. Arms crossed over the chest (or hands relaxed in front).

**Movement:**
1. Lean the chest forward from the hips, nose over toes.
2. Push through the whole foot and stand up fully, hips and knees straight, standing tall.
3. Reverse: push the hips back and bend the knees to sit back down slowly and with control, without dropping.

**Correct form:** back stays long and neutral (hinged at the hips, not rounded). Knees track over the toes. Weight even through both feet. Hands do not push off the thighs or chair.

**Wrong:** rocking or swinging to get up, knees caving in, rounding the back, using the hands to push, plopping down onto the seat, feet lifting.

**Clip:** seated still → stand up → sit down → seated still (2 reps max).

## 11. Step-Up
Pattern: functional · One stair or a sturdy low step (about 15–20 cm / 6–8 in) · One leg shown

**Camera:** side view at hip height, full body including the whole step, so knee and hip line are visible.

**Start position:** standing tall facing the step, close to it, feet hip-width apart. Hands relaxed by the sides (light touch on a railing or wall is acceptable).

**Movement:**
1. Place one whole foot on the step.
2. Press through that foot to stand up tall on the step, bringing the other foot up to meet it.
3. Step back down with the second foot first, then the lead foot, controlled.

**Correct form:** the whole lead foot is on the step. The lead knee tracks over the toes, not inward. Trunk stays upright with a slight forward lean. The lead leg does the work: no push-off from the floor foot.

**Wrong:** the heel hanging off the step, the knee caving in, bouncing off the bottom foot, leaning far forward or hunching, dropping down off the step, a step higher than knee height.

**Clip:** standing still facing the step → 1–2 step-ups with the same lead leg → back on the floor, still.

## 12. 90-90 Breathing (no video — breathing screen)
Pattern: breathing · Floor plus a chair, sofa or wall

Lying on the back with hips and knees both bent at 90°, calves resting on a chair seat (or feet flat on a wall). Arms relaxed, one hand on the stomach, one on the chest. Breathe in slowly through the nose for about 4 seconds so the stomach and lower ribs rise while the chest stays quiet. Breathe out slowly through the mouth for about 6 seconds, letting the ribs drop down. Low back relaxed into the floor. About 60 seconds a set. The breathing screen uses an in-4, out-6 rhythm.
