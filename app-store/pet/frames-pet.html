Meet the armadillo
who grows with you.

Name him. Show up. Watch him uncurl.

A real program,
not a playlist.

Short sessions, planned around you.

Video-guided.
Every exercise.

He coaches from the floor.

Built around
your schedule.

Pick your days. He keeps count.

Starts with
how you feel.

Check in before and after every session.

Poke him too much
and he gets grumpy.

Warm, cheeky, always on your side.

Five stages.
One armadillo.

He uncurls as you finish sessions.

Miss a day?
He waits.

No guilt. He only ever grows.
